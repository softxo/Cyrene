# Security policy

Cyrene stores linked HoYoLAB accounts, so security reports are taken seriously
and handled before anything else.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please don't open a public issue or pull request for a security problem.
Instead, report it privately on the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/KpkggK4UqB)
by messaging a member of Cyrene Staff.

Include:

- what the issue is and what an attacker could do with it;
- steps to reproduce it, or a proof of concept;
- the page, command or endpoint affected.

Don't include real account cookies, Wish History URLs or other people's data.
Test only against your own accounts.

## What to expect

You'll get a reply once a member of staff has looked at the report. Confirmed
issues are fixed on the hosted service first and published here in the next
release. You'll be credited in the release notes unless you'd rather not be.

## Scope

In scope: the website at [cyrene-teyvat.xyz](https://cyrene-teyvat.xyz/), the
Cyrene Discord bot and Cyrene Scanner.

Out of scope: HoYoLAB, Discord and other third-party services, denial of
service, and reports from automated scanners without a demonstrated impact.
