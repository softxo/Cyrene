Import weapon inventory

  1. Download and extract the latest Cyrene Scanner from Cyrene Releases on GitHub.
  2. Open Genshin Impact on Windows in English, windowed, with HDR disabled. Open Inventory → Weapons and clear your filters.
  3. Choose Weapon Scanner, enter your UID and weapon count, calibrate the grid and details panel, then run the scan.
  4. Review the results and save the weapon export. Select the matching account here, then choose your exported JSON file (up to 4 MB).

This replaces the previous scan for this account. Manually added copies stay separate. Equipped weapons are matched with HoYoLAB so they appear once.
