Every page lives in the sidebar on the left. On a phone, open it with the menu button at the top. The main pages sit at the top of the sidebar, and help, account connection and settings sit at the bottom.

Pages that show your own data need a connected Discord account and a linked Genshin account. If something is missing, the page tells you what to connect first. See Connecting your accounts.

{{ topic("account-switching") }}

If you have linked more than one Genshin account, pages that show account data have an account picker near the top. Switching it changes which account the page shows. Most settings, such as automatic code claiming and reminders, are saved per account.

{{ topic("settings") }}

Settings has three areas:

  • Appearance changes the colour palette used across the site.
  • Interface turns the hover effects on or off, and can show announced but unreleased characters. Material data for unreleased content may be incomplete.
  • HoYoLAB Accounts adds, renames and removes linked accounts.
