Characters shows every character on your linked account, synced from HoYoLAB. Filter by element, weapon type and rarity, sort by level, or search by name. Select a character to open their showcase: stats, equipped weapon, artifacts, talents and constellations.

{% call callout("Characters missing or empty?", "tip") %} Make sure your character details are visible in your Genshin Impact Battle Chronicle on HoYoLAB. {% endcall %} {{ topic("planner") }}

The planner works out exactly what a character needs. Add any character, including ones you don't own yet, then set goals for:

  • their level,
  • each talent,
  • and the weapon you plan to give them.

Cyrene totals every material and the Mora required for those goals. Select a goal to see its materials. In Discord, /character-resources and /materials give quick answers for a single character.

{{ topic("weapons") }}

Weapons has three views:

  • Catalogue lists every weapon in the game, with filters for type and rarity.
  • Owned lists the weapons on your account.
  • Planner plans weapon levels and totals the materials.

HoYoLAB only shares weapons that are equipped on a character. To include the rest, add an unequipped copy by hand with Add to Owned, or import your whole inventory with the scanner below.

{{ topic("weapon-scanner") }}

The Cyrene Scanner is a Windows app that reads your weapon inventory from the game.

  1. Download the scanner

    Get the latest Cyrene Scanner from Cyrene Releases on GitHub and extract it.

  2. Prepare the game

    Open Genshin Impact on Windows in English, in windowed mode, with HDR turned off. Go to Inventory → Weapons and clear any filters.

  3. Run the scan

    Choose Weapon Scanner and enter your weapon count. Your UID fills in automatically when Cyrene detects it, and Auto-calibrate sets up the grid and details panel for you. Then start the scan, check the results and save the export.

  4. Import it

    On the Weapons page, press Import, select the matching account and choose the exported JSON file (up to 4 MB).

A new import replaces the previous scan for that account. Weapons you added by hand are kept separately, and equipped weapons are matched with HoYoLAB so they only appear once.
