This policy explains what the Cyrene website and Discord bot store about you, why, and how you can see or delete it. It is written to meet the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

{{ topic("who") }}

Cyrene is an independent, non-commercial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by HoYoverse, Cognosphere or Discord. The person who runs Cyrene is the data controller for the personal data described here. For any privacy question or request, contact us through the Cyrene Discord server or the support panel on this website.

{{ topic("collected") }}

From Discord

  • Your Discord user ID, username, display name and avatar.
  • When you sign in with Discord, the access and refresh tokens Discord issues. Cyrene only asks for the identify permission, which does not include your email address.
  • The ID and name of the server where you linked an account.

From HoYoLAB

  • Your HoYoLAB login cookies and tokens, stored encrypted.
  • Your Genshin UID, server region, in-game nickname and Adventure Rank.
  • Game data fetched for your UID, such as Resin, Expeditions, characters, events and exploration progress.

Game progress stored by UID

Some progress is saved against the Genshin UID rather than your Discord account, so it is kept if you unlink and link the account again: wish history, achievements, character and weapon plans, scanned weapons, event check-offs, import dates, and gift code and daily check-in results.

What you create

  • Account nicknames, reminders, custom events and their progress.
  • Support tickets, including messages and attachments. These are also posted to a private Discord thread so the support team can reply.

Technical data

  • Error reports from the bot, which may include your Discord user, server and channel IDs and the command you ran.
  • Standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, kept by our hosting provider.
{{ topic("not-collected") }}
  • Your HoYoLAB password. When you sign in with a password, it passes through Cyrene's server to sign in to HoYoLAB and is never stored.
  • Wish History URLs and their auth keys. They are used once to import your history and are never stored.
{{ topic("purposes") }}
  • Providing Cyrene (performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)): showing your accounts and game data, sending reminders, claiming gift codes and daily check-ins you turn on, and keeping your plans and progress.
  • Support (performance of a contract): answering the tickets you open.
  • Security and reliability (legitimate interests, Article 6(1)(f)): keeping sign-ins secure, preventing abuse and fixing errors.

Cyrene does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you.

{{ topic("sharing") }}
  • HoYoLAB (HoYoverse): Cyrene sends your login details to HoYoLAB to sign you in and fetch your game data. HoYoLAB's own privacy policy applies to what it does with them.
  • Discord: for signing in, sending bot messages and reminders, and support threads. Discord may process data in the United States.
  • bot-hosting.cloud: hosts the website, the bot and the database.

Cyrene does not share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it. Where data leaves the EU, it relies on the safeguards those services provide, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

{{ topic("retention") }}
  • Your data is kept until you delete it, or until you ask us to. Unlinking an account removes its login cookies but keeps the game progress stored for its UID.
  • Website sign-ins expire after 30 days.
  • After you delete your data, copies in backups are removed when those backups are replaced.
{{ topic("cookies") }}

Cyrene only uses cookies that the site needs to work, so it does not ask for cookie consent:

  • cyrene_session keeps you signed in, for 30 days.
  • cyrene_discord_oauth protects the Discord sign-in, for 10 minutes.
  • cyrene_support links support tickets to your browser, for one year.
  • cyrene_terms remembers that you accepted the Terms of Service, for one year.

Your browser's local storage also keeps preferences such as your theme, hover effects and collapsed sections. This never leaves your device.

{{ topic("rights") }}

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data Cyrene holds about you and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict or object to how your data is used;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To use any of these, contact us through the Discord server. We will reply within one month. You can also complain to the data protection authority in the EU country where you live.

{{ topic("delete") }}

Open Settings and choose Delete my data. This immediately erases:

  • every linked account and its login cookies;
  • your reminders, custom events, nicknames, support tickets;
  • your Discord profile, sign-in tokens and website sessions;
  • game progress for each UID that no other Discord user still has linked.

Your user ID is also removed from error reports. Messages already posted to Discord support threads stay in Discord. Ask on the Discord server if you want those removed too.

{{ topic("security") }}

HoYoLAB login cookies are encrypted before they are stored, sign-in cookies are secure and HTTP-only, and the site only works over HTTPS. Account pages and commands only ever show data for accounts linked to your own Discord account.

{{ topic("policy-changes") }}

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change, and important changes will be announced on the Discord server. Questions are welcome on the Cyrene Discord server.
